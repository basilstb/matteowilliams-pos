Role Boundaries & Permissions Matrix

Explicit definitions of authority, operational permissions, and restrictions across Matteo Williams staff roles in Onitsha.

Frontline Tier

Cashier Role

Responsible for ringing up customer sales, receiving split payments, and collecting debt repayments.

What Cashier CAN Do:
Ring up electronics sales at assigned branch terminal.
Process split payments across Cash, Bank Transfer, POS & Debt.
Accept debt repayment installments and print receipts.
View branch available stock counts.
What Cashier CANNOT Do:
Cannot sell below product minimum floor price.
Cannot issue 100% credit or debt > ₦200k without Manager PIN.
Cannot modify inventory counts without an active sale.
Cannot unlock a 24-hour rate-limited terminal.
Cannot view cost prices or overall owner gross profit.
Assigned: Branch Cashier PIN
Operational Tier

Branch Manager Role

Responsible for branch stock consignments, credit authorizations, and local terminal management.

What Manager CAN Do:
All Cashier functions (POS, split payments, repayments).
Authorize 100% credit or high-debt transactions.
Restock inventory when electronics consignments arrive.
Emergency unlock branch terminal if locked by 3 failed PINs.
View branch daily cash drawer and transfer settlements.
What Manager CANNOT Do:
Cannot create or delete branches in other markets.
Cannot change the General Gateway password.
Cannot view other branches' sensitive financial totals.
Cannot alter audit logs.
Assigned: Branch Manager PIN
Executive Tier

Business Owner Role

Full superuser control over all 3+ Onitsha branches, financials, branch expansion, and credentials.

Owner Sovereign Powers:
Establish new branches at any Onitsha or Nigerian location.
Configure & reassign all Cashier and Manager PINs.
Set and update the General Business Gateway password.
Emergency unlock any locked terminal across the network.
Consolidated cross-branch revenue and profit analytics.
Inspect the comprehensive immutable security audit trail.
Master Owner PIN: 0000
